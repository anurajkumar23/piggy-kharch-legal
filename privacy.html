Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Piggy Kharch mobile app ("Piggy Kharch", "we") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile picture. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.
  • Data you enter: expenses, income, budgets, bills, savings goals, shopping lists, and the names and relationships of household members you add.
  • Household membership: which family spaces you create or join, and your role in them.
  • Technical data: basic information needed to run the app, such as sign-in session tokens stored on your device.

We do not collect your contacts, location, SMS, or bank account details, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your records on your phone (so the app works offline) and sync them to your account.
  • To share household records with the members of a family space you created or joined.
  • To show reminders and alerts (for example bill due dates and budget limits) on your device.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google user data

When you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them solely to create and identify your Piggy Kharch account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with anyone except as needed to provide sign-in.

4. Who can see your data

  • You: your personal space is visible only to you.
  • Your family space: records in a shared household are visible to its members. If you share a view-only monitor code, the people who use it can view your personal expenses but cannot change them.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to store data and handle sign-in, and Google for Google Sign-In. They process data only to provide these services to us. Access to data in our database is restricted by row-level security rules so that users can only read records of spaces they belong to.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on your device and in our cloud database, and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and your data at any time from Settings → Delete My Account & All Data in the app. This removes your cloud records and the data stored on your phone. You can also email us to request deletion, and we will process it within 30 days.

7. Children

Piggy Kharch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • You can view, edit or delete your records in the app at any time.
  • If you are the admin of a family space, you can remove members or delete the space.
  • You can turn off notifications in the app settings or your phone settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
