Piggy Kharch

Personal & family expense tracker for Indian households

Piggy Kharch helps you and your family record daily spending, set budgets, track bills and savings goals, and see where the money goes. It works fully offline on your phone and syncs with your family's shared space when you are online.

⚡ Offline-first

Add expenses without internet. They sync automatically when you are back online.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family spaces

Invite family members with a code and track household spending together.

💰 Budgets & bills

Category budgets, bill reminders and savings goals in one place.

🌐 3 languages

English, Hindi and Hinglish.

Sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account or with email and password. Piggy Kharch uses Google Sign-In only to get your name, email address and profile picture so it can create your account.
